# Oversized surface proteins — survey and design record

The 99 genes that clear every library gate except the ~5 kb ORF cap.
`OVERSIZED_SURVEY.tsv` has one row per gene joining everything below; this
explains what the columns mean and what was decided.

## Where the number comes from

**99, not 107.** The 107 in `library/l1/surface-filters/`
predates library changes and no longer matches. The authoritative list is
regenerated by re-running that experiment's oversized branch
(`final_library._passes_except_orf` and not `orf_ok`) and is cached here as
`oversized_authoritative.json`. Its prose H/M/L triage is also illustrative
rather than exhaustive — about a third of the set is not named anywhere in it,
which is why NOTCH4 went untriaged while NOTCH1, 2 and 3 were graded H.

## Coverage

All 99 carry a triage status and a topology measurement. 36 have a concrete
design — a built construct, a cleavage-derived fragment, or both. 63 have only
status and topology, and 27 of those are deliberate skips.

| status | n |
|---|---|
| skip (channels, brush-border enzymes, structural adhesion) | 27 |
| built | 23 |
| sufficient — can spec now | 15 |
| needs-deeper-search | 11 |
| ready to build on an existing family recipe | 9 |
| needs a first-principles look | 9 |
| expression unknown | 3 |
| not viable at this budget / no displayable face | 2 |

## The budget

p0039's non-ORF load is 4,087 bp LTR-to-LTR. With the tag cassette and barcode
that leaves **1,535 aa** at a 9 kb ceiling. Measure against the tagged
construct, not the pre-tag intermediate — that is 173 bp it is easy to forget.

## Six strategies, and when each applies

The determining question is what the ectodomain is made of, not how big it is.

| ectodomain | route | examples |
|---|---|---|
| tandem repeat array | delete interior repeats | CDH23, PCDH15, CSMDs |
| discrete folded units | keep one whole unit, drop the others | LRP1, LRP2, NOTCH NRR |
| one co-folded shell | cannot cut inside | teneurins, ABCA1 |
| naturally cleaved | use the fragment the cell makes | PKD1, PTPRs, CELSRs, mucins |
| natural short isoform | use it | MUC4/Y, CR1 alleles, PTPRZ1 |
| cytoplasmic tail only | truncate the tail | ITGB4 |

Two rules ride along everywhere: a leader must survive (native, or Igκ where the
fragment starts mid-protein), and at least 30 residues must sit between signal
cleavage and the transmembrane span or the TM acts as a signal anchor and
inverts the topology.

## Things that cost time, recorded so they do not again

* **Fitting the budget is not being displayable.** Rank by retained
  extracellular residues, not by whether the ORF fits.
* **Count ectodomain across all extracellular segments.** Measuring only the
  stretch before TM1 reported PKD1 as 26 residues when it keeps 338 — its TOP
  domain sits between transmembrane spans.
* **UniProt writes the outside face as "Lumenal" on secretory-pathway
  proteins.** Matching only "Extracellular" reported SORL1's 2,056-residue
  ectodomain as zero.
* **A CHAIN starting at residue 20 is the mature protein, not a cut.** LRP1 and
  SI were both reported as shortened on that.
* **The smallest annotated chain may be soluble.** NOTCH1's is the intracellular
  domain.
* **A cleavage boundary is a candidate, not an answer.** For NOTCH it yields the
  NEXT form, which sheds; the literature NRR design keeps ~290 residues more.
* **Motif scans need calibrating against chance.** A bare furin consensus has
  10.9% precision against UniProt; the class-I PDZ motif is only 2.85-fold
  enriched, so it reports "at risk" rather than forbidden.

## What is still open

1. Nine genes need a first-principles look: CSMD1/2/3, PKHD1 (now resolved by
   cleavage), MEGF8, TENM1/2/4, FNDC1. The teneurins are a single β-barrel shell
   with no interior to remove; FNDC1 has no transmembrane span at all and
   probably should not be in the set.
2. Nine inherit a family recipe already built and need no new literature:
   NOTCH4, PTPRB, PTPRQ, LRP1B, FAT3, DCHS1, MUC22, ADGRV1, GPR179.
3. Three have no expression data in any source: MUC3B, PTPRZ2, ADGRG4.
4. PLXNA3 and PLXNB3 rest on inference with no truncation precedent of their
   own. The single document that would settle PLXNA3 is the Methods of Cheng
   2001, Neuron 32:249 — not in PMC, and three agents could not retrieve it.
